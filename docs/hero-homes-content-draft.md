## 0. Read first

### 0A. RERA registration ⚠️

The poster says **"RERA expected in 3 weeks"**, so the project isn't registered yet. Under the RERA Act, 2016, a project generally can't be advertised, marketed or booked, by the developer or by a channel partner, until it's registered. Once registered, every listing has to show the RERA registration number and the authority's website.

**Recommendation:** publish sections 1–5 (descriptive content) only with the client's OK, and hold section 6 (price, offers, booking, payment plan) until the RERA number is issued. When it is, the number goes on the project page and the card.

> Leafsong Retreat is Hero Realty's new address in Sector MU, Greater Noida: three G+31 towers of premium 3 BHK residences, with only six apartments on each floor. Homes come in four plans from 1,656 to 2,289 sq ft, each planned around light, privacy and room to breathe.

A podium level adds a lap pool, kids' pool, Jacuzzi and a pool-deck café.
